## 1. The story in six lines

1. **The original hypothesis was rejected for Lorenz-63** (October 2026,
   `docs/results.md`). It said that a residual's alignment with the leading
   Lyapunov or singular direction predicts later error beyond the residual's
   size. A later fresh-window repeat on another machine gave the same answer
   (*inherited*, `docs/validation_progress_2026-11-22.md`, not re-run here).
2. **This session asked when direction can help.** Theory needs three things:
   - a large stretching gap,
   - residual directions that vary,
   - a target that the residual actually creates.
3. **Two pre-registered studies tested that.**
   - The **dial study** gave the verdict "partly supported": direction helps at
     large gaps and not at Lorenz-like gaps.
   - The **reference-free study** gave the verdict "supported": directions
     computed from the PINN's own path give the same gain as the oracle on
     fresh starting points.
4. **Why Lorenz-63 fails.** It sits below the threshold. A PINN residual keeps
   its direction for only about 0.1 time units, and over that time Lorenz
   barely separates directions.
5. **Stiff systems are the natural case where direction helps.** A residual
   pointing into a fast-decaying direction looks large but creates almost no
   error.
6. **Effect sizes:** +0.07 to +0.14 in held-out R² for the error created over
   the next 0.1 time units. There is little or no gain for the total future
   error.

## 3. Key results (verified in this session)

### Dial study

Primary test at horizon 0.1:
- **Target:** log of the error created inside the horizon,
  N = ||e(t+dt) − Phi(t+dt, t) e(t)||.
- **Baseline B:** log||r||, tau and tau².
- **Model M2:** B + log|r·s₁|, with an oracle s₁.
- **Evaluation:** leave-one-starting-point-out, with 95% intervals from
  resampling whole starting points.

| setting (rate difference = dial) | median sigma_1/sigma_2 | gain (95% interval) | planned verdict |
|---|---:|---|---|
| saddle, kappa = 1 (2) | 1.22 | +0.005 (−0.005, +0.013) | fails |
| saddle, kappa = 4 (5) | 1.65 | +0.001 (−0.004, +0.006) | fails |
| saddle, kappa = 16 (17) | 5.47 | +0.026 (+0.002, +0.043) | inconclusive |
| saddle, kappa = 64 (65) | 665 | +0.092 (+0.062, +0.131) | works |
| Davis–Skodje, gamma = 3 (2) | 1.25 | −0.000 | fails |
| Davis–Skodje, gamma = 6 (5) | 1.71 | +0.003 | fails |
| Davis–Skodje, gamma = 18 (17) | 5.95 | +0.072 (+0.049, +0.095) | works |
| Davis–Skodje, gamma = 66 (65) | 746 | +0.128 (+0.100, +0.159) | inconclusive (degenerate control) |
| Lorenz-63, October runs (reference) | 1.77 | −0.012 (−0.024, −0.007) | – |

**Verdicts.**
- **Family L is supported** (Kendall's tau 0.67).
- **Family D is not supported:** its top setting missed the time-shuffle
  control. The shuffled gain was 0.129 against the real 0.128, because s₁ turns
  0.0° within a run at gamma = 66, so the control is degenerate.
- **Overall: "partly supported".**

### Reference-free study

The verdict is decided on fresh starting points 9–16.
- **P** (tangent propagator along the PINN's own path, reference-free) gives
  +0.095 (saddle 64), +0.065 (Davis–Skodje 18) and +0.137 (Davis–Skodje 66).
- These equal the oracle gains: 100% recovery, paired difference 0.0000.
- The control (saddle 1) gives −0.004.
- **Verdict: "supported".**

Angle between the P direction and the oracle direction:

| case | median | 90th percentile |
|---|---:|---:|
| Davis–Skodje (fresh windows) | ≤ 0.0003° | – |
| Lorenz-63 | 0.8° | 18.6° |
| Lorenz-63, F (frozen Jacobian) | 6.5° | 45.7° |

### Insights worth reusing

1. **Residual direction persists for only about 0.1 time units.** In the
   Lorenz PINNs, the mean signed cosine between r(t) and r(t+lag) is 0.52 at
   lag 0.05, 0.51 at 0.1, 0.15 at 0.25 and −0.02 at 0.5. That is why the
   direction effect appears at dt = 0.05–0.1 and vanishes by 0.25–0.5, even
   where sigma_1/sigma_2 is about 10¹⁴.
2. **For a continuous residual the real lever is much smaller than
   sigma_1/sigma_2.** It is the ratio of the singular values of the integrated
   propagator, K = ∫ Phi(t+dt, s) ds over s from t to t+dt. At dial 65 and
   dt = 0.1, sigma_1/sigma_2 is about 700 but the lever is only about 6.7.
3. **Use the created error N as the target.** The total future error is
   dominated by inherited error and mostly hides direction effects.
4. **The time-shuffle control is degenerate whenever s₁ does not turn in
   time.** This applies to the linear saddle and to Davis–Skodje at large
   gamma.
5. **A soft initial condition suits stiff transients.** The hard form
   x0 + (t/T)(g − x0) forces the raw output g to spike to about T·kappa·d near
   t = 0.
6. **The tangent integrator along a PINN path must be robust.** An inaccurate
   path can cross a singularity of the equations (Davis–Skodje at y1 = −1).
   `pinn_path_step_propagators` therefore uses exponential midpoint steps.
7. **In Lorenz-63, the T = 1 oracle gain for ||Phi r|| disappears once
   elapsed time is in the baseline.** This was an exploratory check here, not
   committed as a script. The T = 2 sequential-PINN gains (inherited) survive
   the time control.

## 4. Decisions and why

- **Stiffness turns the dial**, not expanding saddles such as a pendulum near
  its top. Persistence limits the useful horizon to about 0.1, so the gap must
  be large at short horizons. Contraction delivers that without an explosive
  dynamic range.
- **The primary target is N and the baseline includes time.** This isolates
  what the residual creates and removes the elapsed-time confound.
- **The training settings were amended before any training:** a soft
  initial condition with lambda = 100, and output scaling from all nine
  reference windows. The reasons are in the plan.
- **All 320 dial runs were trained on the GPU** as stacked models.
  - **Why:** a 10-process CPU pool on this machine was about 7× slower per
    run.
  - **What happened to the CPU runs:** the 20 completed CPU runs of
    Davis–Skodje gamma = 3 were set aside unused, in the session scratchpad,
    which may already be cleared.
- **Post-hoc checks are labelled and never change a verdict.** They are the
  late-time sensitivity, the accurate-runs-only check and the reading of the
  shuffle control.
- **P uses exponential midpoint steps.** This was decided after the adaptive
  integrator failed on one development run (gamma = 3, window 7, seed 4, whose
  path reached y1 = −5.3), before any fresh result was seen.

## 6. How to reproduce (environment notes)

**Environments.** Use `.venv` (CPU) for tests and analysis and `.venv-cuda`
(torch cu130) for GPU training. Both live in the repository directory and are
gitignored.

**Commands, in order.** These regenerate the gitignored arrays.

```bash
.venv/bin/python experiments/24_dial_reference.py
setsid nohup .venv-cuda/bin/python experiments/25_dial_pinn_runs.py --iterations 20000 --device cuda > logs/dial.log 2>&1 &
.venv/bin/python experiments/26_dial_analysis.py
.venv/bin/python experiments/24_dial_reference.py --out results/reference_free/reference \
    --settings saddle_k1 saddle_k64 davis_skodje_g18 davis_skodje_g66 --windows 9 10 11 12 13 14 15 16
setsid nohup .venv-cuda/bin/python experiments/25_dial_pinn_runs.py --iterations 20000 --device cuda \
    --settings saddle_k1 saddle_k64 davis_skodje_g18 davis_skodje_g66 --windows 9 10 11 12 13 14 15 16 \
    --reference-dir results/reference_free/reference --out results/reference_free > logs/rf.log 2>&1 &
.venv/bin/python experiments/27_reference_free_analysis.py
```

**Run times on this machine.** One 40-run setting takes about 7.5 min on the
GPU. The full dial study took about 1.5 h, the reference-free training about
30 min, and the two analyses 0.5–1 min each.

**Gotchas.**
- **Avoid big CPU pools here.** Ten CPU worker processes ran about 7× slower
  per run than a single run, so prefer GPU stacking.
- **`pkill -f "<pattern>"` can kill your own shell,** because the shell's
  command line contains the pattern. Use a bracket trick such as
  `pkill -f "[2]5_dial..."`.
- **zsh rejects `tail -6 a b`.** Use `tail -n 6`.
- **Background jobs** need `setsid nohup ... &`. Logs go to `logs/`, which is
  gitignored.
